دليل حدائق الأهرام — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0. Updated October 11, 2026. Developed by Mohammed Salah. Package: com.taasham.hadaiqguide. This community guide is not an official district app.

Accounts and location

No account is required. The app uses foreground location to display your position, calculate distances and sort nearby listings. You can deny permission and continue browsing. Coordinates are not uploaded to a developer-owned server; background location is not requested.

External maps and links

OpenStreetMap and Esri tiles are requested over the internet. Providers receive your IP address and tile requests describing the displayed area and may keep logs under their own policies. Opening Google Maps, a source website or WhatsApp transfers you to an external service. Navigation links can include destination coordinates. We do not claim all external services are tracking-free.

Local storage and deletion

Appearance, language, recent searches, suggestion drafts and local edits are stored on your device. Search history and drafts can be cleared in the app. Clear Android app data to remove all local data; uninstalling or clearing data also removes local edits. Automatic Android backup is disabled for this release. There is no developer-held user account or personal database to delete.

Ads and fonts

Ads are disabled in 1.0.0. The app does not initialize the advertising SDK or request ads, and advertising-ID permission is removed. Fonts are bundled without external font requests. Enabling ads in a future release requires updated disclosures and any applicable consent configuration.

Contact, sharing and directory information

Suggestion drafts stay local until you choose to open WhatsApp. Messages are not sent automatically. App sharing opens the system share sheet; you choose the app and recipient. Directory contacts and addresses are publicly sourced information, not browsing-time user data. Original records are retained; display spellings may be transliterated and are not necessarily official business names. Boundaries and some positions are approximate.
